## Brand & Style

### Personality & Purpose
The design system establishes an institutional, regulatory, and industrial-grade digital environment designed for water verification units under the Mexican standard NMX-AA-179-SCFI-2018 (CONAGUA). It operates with the uncompromising rigor of legal, metrological, and administrative compliance. The interface prioritizes legal certitude, operational efficiency, high-density legibility, and audit transparency over decorative trends.

### Aesthetic Tone
- **Technical & Institutional:** Grounded in authoritative marine slate, petroleum teal, and crisp white surfaces.
- **Utilitarian Clarity:** Information density is treated as a core capability; data tables, split inspectors, status flags, and metrological folios are structured without ambiguous ornamentation.
- **Operational Ergonomics:** Designed for high-stress administrative review on desktop workstations and rugged, outdoor field data capture on mobile touch devices.

### Design Movement
**Modern Industrial / Technical Corporate**: Flat, crisp border architectures; high color-contrast ratio; monospace micro-typographic accents; deliberate avoidance of blur filters, soft neomorphic depths, or non-functional ambient shadows. Structural boundaries are reinforced via 1px border lines, structured grids, and unambiguous state tokens.

## Colors

### Architectural Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#0B6470` - Petroleum Accent):** The action engine. Dictates interactive targets, primary buttons, active workflow tabs, progress trackers, and normative focus states.
- **Secondary (`#12303A` - Deep Marine / Shell):** The organizational anchor. Confined to structural navigation, master sidebars, device shells, and permanent administrative framing.
- **Tertiary (`#2A5866` - Marine Slate):** Secondary control structures, badge borders, brand identity blocks, and track divisions.
- **Neutral (`#15212B` - Regulatory Ink):** Maximum-contrast text ink for body content, tabular data, field headers, and terminal rules.

### Normative Semantic States
Color signaling is strict, functional, and non-negotiable for audit verification:
- **Conformity / Approved (`#1C5B3A` text on `#DDF0E6` surface):** Dictates compliant meters, verified calibrations, approved certificates, and active validations.
- **Alert / In-Review (`#8A3E0B` text on `#FBE7D3` surface, `#F0CFAE` border):** Unverified files, pending calibrations, expiring documents, and attention-required items.
- **Non-Conformity / Expired (`#8E2A1C` text on `#FBE0DC` surface, `#EDB9B1` border):** Critical SLA violations, failed audit checks, expired certifications, and invalid sensor telemetry.
- **Operational / Scheduled (`#0B4F59` text on `#E3EEF1` surface):** Scheduled inspections, pending site visits, and assigned technician states.

## Typography

### Typographic Hierarchy
The typographic system pairs the structural clarity of **Public Sans** with the programmatic precision of **IBM Plex Mono**. 

- **Primary Interface (Public Sans):** Chosen for its exceptional legibility across dense screens, institutional tone, and robust number rendering. It is utilized across all views, headers, tabular bodies, control states, and navigational menus.
- **Monospace Technical Data (IBM Plex Mono):** Strictly reserved for legal identifiers, verification folios (`[UVA-0001]`), CONAGUA concession titles, QR payload strings, GPS coordinates, serial numbers, and file tree paths. It reinforces metrological authenticity and eliminates character confusion (`0` vs `O`, `1` vs `l`).
- **Overline / Category Labels:** Fixed at `11px`, bold, uppercase, with `0.08em` tracking. Used for section dividers in the dark sidebar, metrological column groups, and metric tile headers.

## Layout & Spacing

### Layout Structure
The system relies on an asymmetric split workspace:
- **Primary Shell:** A fixed, persistent 264px navigation rail (`#12303A`) pinned to the left edge at 100vh.
- **Data Workspace:** A fluid data canvas (`#F4F6F8`) with interior content constraints.
- **Master-Detail Splitting:** Main table/editor views scale fluidly (`flex: 999 1 560px`), paired with an inspector/evidence drawer (`flex: 1 1 340px`, max 420px) to enable uninterrupted inspection workflows without context switching.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** Full split layout with visible inspector rails, fluid 4 or 5-column metric summaries, and comprehensive data table columns. Outer margin: `2rem` (32px).
- **Tablet (901px – 1279px):** Inspector rails collapse into slide-over panels; metric cards wrap to 2 or 3 columns. Data tables enable horizontal scrolling with pinned key identifiers. Outer margin: `1.5rem` (24px).
- **Mobile / Field Inspector (≤ 900px):** Layout converts to a single vertical column. The 264px sidebar switches to an off-canvas drawer or collapsible app bar. Table records morph into actionable card units with touch targets of at least 44px. Outer margin: `1rem` (16px).

## Elevation & Depth

### Philosophy: Structural Flatness & Border Hierarchy
This system deliberately rejects ambient diffuse shadows, colorful glows, and blurred surfaces. In industrial and regulatory environments, depth must communicate hierarchy and state, not visual fantasy.

- **Canvas Base (`#F4F6F8`):** The ground plane. Never contains interactive controls directly without a card or panel wrapper.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Every card, table, modal, and control rests on pure white. Depth is expressed exclusively through a crisp 1px border (`#DDE3E8`).
- **Internal Division:** Nested elements, row borders, and list dividers use a subtler 1px hairline (`#E4E9ED`).
- **Active State Elevation:** Achieved through surface color shift (`#F3F9FA`) and a 2px active border accent (`#0B6470`), rather than z-axis drop shadows.
- **Overlay & Modal Elevation:** Modals and field inspection viewports use a controlled, dark neutral shadow: `0 10px 30px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.15)`. Backdrop scrims use `#12303A` at 60% opacity to preserve brand identity without muddying content.

## Shapes

### Shape Language & Geometry
The interface utilizes an engineered geometric profile (`roundedness: 2`, base radius 8px) that strikes an exact balance between modern operational software and structural institutional forms.

- **Base Controls (8px):** Form inputs, buttons, dropdown triggers, callout containers, and image attachment blocks.
- **Structural Panels & Cards (10px):** Verification modules, summary tiles, data table viewports, and audit dossiers.
- **Sub-Controls & Navigation (6px):** Sidebar interactive links, inner-table inline actions, segmented tabs, and progress track ends.
- **Micro-Indicators (4px):** Technical tags, metrological calibration counters, and code blocks.
- **Full Rounded Pills (999px):** Exclusively reserved for status badges ("Conforme", "No Conforme", "En Revisión") and circular toggle pills.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid `#0B6470` fill with white `#FFFFFF` text. Height: 40px (desktop) / 48px (field mobile). Border-radius: 8px. Hover: `#084C55`. Active: `#063940`. Focus: 2px offset ring in `#0B6470`.
- **Secondary Action:** White `#FFFFFF` fill with 1px solid `#C4CED5` border, `#15212B` text. Hover: `#F4F6F8` with `#A9B6BF` border.
- **Destructive Action:** White fill with 1px solid `#EDB9B1`, text in `#8E2A1C`. Hover: `#FBE0DC`.
- **Disabled State:** Solid `#C9D3D9` fill, white `#FFFFFF` text, pointer-events none, zero shadow.

### 2. Status Chips & Badges
- **Shape:** Pill-shaped (`border-radius: 999px`), padding: 2px 10px, typography: `label-sm` (`12px`, weight 600).
- **Conforme (Compliant):** Background `#DDF0E6`, text `#1C5B3A`, border: 1px solid `#1C5B3A`.
- **Pendiente / Alerta (Warning):** Background `#FBE7D3`, text `#8A3E0B`, border: 1px solid `#F0CFAE`.
- **No Conforme (Non-Compliant):** Background `#FBE0DC`, text `#8E2A1C`, border: 1px solid `#EDB9B1`.
- **Trámite / Programada (Operational):** Background `#E3EEF1`, text `#0B4F59`, border: 1px solid `#CFE3E7`.

### 3. Form Input Fields
- **Container:** Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with 1px solid `#C4CED5` border. Height: 40px, padding: 0 12px. Border-radius: 8px.
- **Typography:** `body-md` (`14px`, `#15212B`). Placeholder text in `#5A6B76`.
- **Focus State:** 2px border in `#0B6470` with zero blur glow.
- **Error State:** 2px border in `#8E2A1C` with descriptive error text beneath using `label-sm` in `#8E2A1C`.
- **Technical Inputs:** Numeric flow rates, calibration coefficients, and folios automatically inherit `code-md` (`IBM Plex Mono`).

### 4. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Checkboxes:** 18x18px square, border: 1px solid `#C4CED5`, radius: 4px. Checked state: solid `#0B6470` fill with white SVG checkmark.
- **Normative Evaluation Radios:** Large tap targets (min 44px height). Default: border 1px solid `#C4CED5`. Selected "Conforme": border 2px solid `#1C5B3A`, background `#DDF0E6`. Selected "No Conforme": border 2px solid `#8E2A1C`, background `#FBE0DC`.

### 5. Data Tables
- **Container:** Surface `#FFFFFF` enclosed in 1px solid `#DDE3E8` border, radius 10px, zero outer shadow.
- **Header (`thead`):** Surface `#EEF2F5`, border-bottom 1px solid `#DDE3E8`. Text in `label-sm` (`#3E4F5A`, weight 600, uppercase). Height: 40px.
- **Row (`tr`):** Border-bottom 1px solid `#E4E9ED`. Height: 48px standard, 36px in compact metrology mode.
- **Row Interaction:** Hover: `#F4F6F8`. Selected/Active: `#F3F9FA` with a 2px left border accent in `#0B6470`.

### 6. Cards & Metric Tiles (KPIs)
- **Container:** Pure white surface, 1px solid `#DDE3E8`, border-radius 10px, padding: 16px 20px.
- **Content Hierarchy:** Overline label (`11px`, `#5A6B76`, uppercase) followed by a high-contrast metric counter (`30px` or `34px`, `#15212B`, weight 700), paired with micro-trend badges and audit status summaries.

### 7. Additional Domain-Specific Components
- **Normative Evidence Dropzone:** Enclosed in a 1px dashed border (`#A9B6BF`), background `#F4F6F8`, 8px radius. Supports camera capture integration on field devices.
- **Folio Tag:** Monospace block (`IBM Plex Mono`, `13px`) wrapped in `#EEF2F5` with `#DDE3E8` border, indicating audit traceability.
- **Calibration Warning Banner:** Callout banner with `#FDF3E9` background, `#F0CFAE` border, `#8A3E0B` icon and title, with `#6B3209` body text, signaling expired equipment calibration.
